What a restaurant content calendar looks like over 30 days

A calendar only helps if your team can follow it with a phone and a few spare minutes. So we plan it around what already happens in your restaurant, in a weekly rhythm:

  • Signature dishes: the plate guests come back for, and where it comes from.
  • Kitchen moments: broth going on in the morning, herbs being washed, spring rolls being rolled.
  • The people: the owner, the cook, the server regulars ask for by name.
  • Specials: a weekend dish, a seasonal menu, a Tết or Mid-Autumn offer.
  • Customer questions: is it spicy, is there a vegetarian option, how do you eat it.

Each slot says what to film and what the caption should cover. Your team supplies the photos and videos, because we do not do shoots. For more, read our restaurant Instagram content ideas and the guide to an AI content calendar for small business.

Restaurant social media management: we build it, your team runs it

Many owners look for someone to take over the restaurant's pages. We work differently: we do not post every day on your behalf, run ads or answer messages for you. We set up the system and your team runs it, because the person who knows the bánh xèo batter was just mixed is in your kitchen, not in our studio.

The inbox follows the same rule. Automatic replies to reservation and opening-hours questions cover what has a fixed answer, then point guests to the phone number or booking link you already use. What each platform allows differs and can change, so we set up what your accounts offer.

This is an add-on to our brand and marketing for restaurants, because the assistant needs a clear story and voice to learn from. See also the general page on social media automation.

Questions, answered

Do you manage our Facebook and Instagram pages for us?

No. We build the system and your team runs it: they take the photos, approve the drafts and answer the messages that need a person.

Does it connect to our reservation or delivery software?

We do not promise that. The inbox replies answer common questions and send guests to the phone number or booking link you already use.

Are the captions in English and Vietnamese?

Yes. The assistant drafts in both, in the voice we define with you, and you choose which language each post uses.

What if the AI gets a dish wrong?

Nothing is published without approval. AI does the fast work and people make the decisions: you can edit or reject every draft.

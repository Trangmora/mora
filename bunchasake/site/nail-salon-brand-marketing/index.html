Nail salon branding: what customers see before they book

Most new customers meet your salon on a phone. They look at photos, read a few reviews, check prices and decide quickly. Only later do they see the sign and the menu at the front desk.

Nail salon branding means those moments tell one story. In practice:

  • A name and logo people can read from the road and remember when a friend asks where they had their nails done.
  • Colours and type used the same way on the sign, the menu, the cards and your posts.
  • A voice: how you greet people, describe a service and answer a review.
  • A clear menu, grouped the way customers ask for services.

Social media for nail salons: we set it up, your team runs it

We are honest about the model. We do not post for you every day, run ads or do photo shoots. The best content is already in your technicians' hands: a finished set, filmed on a phone in good light.

We build the system around those photos. The brand work gives you post templates in your colours. The automation add-on adds a 30-day calendar and captions drafted in natural English, with Vietnamese where your customers use it.

You explain things to us in Vietnamese, as you would to a friend. We turn that into English that sounds like a real salon, not a translation.

How a Vietnamese marketing agency for overseas businesses works with you

We are a Vietnamese studio working between Vietnam and Italy, online with clients anywhere. The first conversation, 60 minutes long, can be in Vietnamese or English. You tell the story the way you would tell a friend. As a Vietnamese brand agency, our job is to turn it into a brand your local customers understand too.

There are three packages:

  • Start, for a new business: lean positioning, logo, colours and type, business card, avatar and post templates.
  • Build: all six steps, with brand story and voice, brand guidelines, website and key touchpoints.
  • Refresh: we audit the current brand, reposition it, refresh the identity and update the touchpoints in use.

We also have pages on brand and marketing for nail salons and brand and marketing for restaurants.

What bilingual Vietnamese English marketing means in practice

Vietnamese diaspora businesses often serve two groups at once. Older Vietnamese customers search in Vietnamese and trust word of mouth. Younger and local customers search in English, look at photos and read reviews before they come. The brand has to work for both.

Bilingual does not mean translating line by line. Each language is written naturally, with the same name, the same promise and the same look. Along the way we decide with you:

  • Which language leads on the sign, the menu or price list, and the website.
  • Which words stay in Vietnamese, and which need a short explanation.
  • How the brand answers messages and reviews in each language.

Once the voice is clear, a social media automation system helps your team post every week without starting from zero.

Six steps, and you approve after each

Step 1

Listening

A 60-minute conversation to hear the founder’s story: why you started, what you believe in and what you hope for.

Step 2

Positioning

Who the brand is, who it is for, how it differs. We analyse the competitors around you in depth to see where you stand and which way to go, guided by what the founder wants, then write the story and key messages.

Step 3

Identity

We work directly with the founder and the person in charge of the brand: a name if needed, logo, colours, typography and voice, agreed part by part in guidelines built for the long term.

Step 4

Reach

Once customers start to recognise the brand, we take it where they go: website, signage or packaging, business cards, social media (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, LinkedIn, Zalo, Pinterest) and the media they follow. For existing channels, we read the data, customer behaviour and past campaign results, then advise on new campaigns.

Step 5

Community

A brand that grows without a community behind it is not yet a success. Returning customers from a good community save a lot of sales and marketing spend, so we help you build one on the platforms your customers prefer, for loyal customers.

Step 6

Crisis communication

Every business meets a communication crisis at some point. Our team, specialised in studying and handling crises, keeps the impact and risk to your brand to a minimum and trains your team to respond like communication professionals.

Questions, answered

Do you write in both English and Vietnamese?

Yes. Both are our working languages, and we define the brand voice in each. See our guide to bilingual marketing for Vietnamese businesses abroad.

You are in Vietnam and Italy. What about my time zone?

We work online with clients anywhere. We agree a time for the conversation that suits both sides, and you review each step when the shop is quiet.

Who posts on Facebook, TikTok and Instagram?

Your own team. We do not run ads or post for you every day. We set up the system: AI drafts, you approve, posts go out on schedule.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the package: Start, Build or Refresh. The quote comes after the first conversation.

I already have a logo and a sign. Do I start over?

No. The Refresh package audits and refreshes the brand you already have, so you do not start from zero.

What holds a Vietnamese brand back abroad

It sells well at home. Abroad, nobody replies.

A buyer in Europe or the US has never heard of you. Without a clear story and a credible look, the email gets skipped.

Our English was translated word for word.

The grammar may be right while the tone is not. What sounds respectful in Vietnamese can read as vague in English.

Our name and packaging only make sense in Vietnam.

A name that is hard to say, or a design full of local references, asks the buyer to do extra work.

Why work with a Vietnamese brand agency for international markets

We are a Vietnamese studio working between Vietnam and Italy. We know how a founder in Vietnam tells the story, and we see how a customer in Europe hears it. That view from both sides is what cross-border marketing from Vietnam often lacks.

Before any design work, we look at the brand the way a buyer abroad would:

  • Can they say and remember the name?
  • Does the story answer: why buy from a company I have never heard of?
  • Does the identity hold up next to the brands already on that shelf?
  • Does the website answer their first questions in clear English?

For a Vietnam to Europe market entry, have these answers ready before the first trade fair. See our article on how Vietnamese brands can enter European markets.

English content for Vietnamese brands, written for the buyer

English content for Vietnamese brands usually starts as a translation. The words are correct, but the page still reads like a brochure for the home market.

Take a furniture maker in Bình Dương selling to Germany. High quality, reasonable price, prestige tells the buyer nothing. Which wood, who makes it, what they can count on: that is a voice a buyer remembers.

So we set the key messages during positioning, then write the brand voice in English and Vietnamese: how the brand introduces itself, which words it uses and avoids, how formal it is. Your team applies it on the website, company profile, packaging and in the social media automation system.

Six steps, and you approve after each

Step 1

Listening

A 60-minute conversation to hear the founder’s story: why you started, what you believe in and what you want, then we listen to customers in the new market too.

Step 2

Positioning

Who the brand is in the new market, who it is for, how it differs. We analyse competitors there in depth to see where you stand and which way to go, guided by what the founder wants, then write the story and key messages.

Step 3

Identity

We work directly with the founder and the person in charge of the brand: a name if needed, logo, colours, typography and voice, agreed part by part in guidelines built for the long term.

Step 4

Reach

Once customers start to recognise the brand, we take it where they go: website, packaging, company profile, social media (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, LinkedIn, Zalo, Pinterest) and the media they follow. For existing channels, we read the data, customer behaviour and past campaign results, then advise on new campaigns.

Step 5

Community

A brand that grows without a community behind it is not yet a success. Returning customers from a good community save a lot of sales and marketing spend, so we help you build one on the platforms your customers prefer, for loyal customers.

Step 6

Crisis communication

Every business meets a communication crisis at some point. Our team, specialised in studying and handling crises, keeps the impact and risk to your brand to a minimum and trains your team to respond like communication professionals.

Questions, answered

We are in Vietnam. How do we work with you?

Online or in person. You email us, we have a 60-minute conversation, then you receive a proposal and quote.

Do you write in English or in Vietnamese?

Both. We listen to the founder in Vietnamese and write the brand voice for buyers in English.

Are you an international marketing agency in Vietnam that runs ads abroad?

No. We do not run ads, book influencers or arrange distribution. We build the brand those efforts rely on.

We already have a logo and packaging. Must we start again?

Not necessarily. The Refresh package audits the current brand, repositions it for the new market, and refreshes the identity and touchpoints.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the package: Start, Build or Refresh. The quote comes after the first conversation.

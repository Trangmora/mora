Refunds and cancellations

Last updated 11 Nov 2026

Each Bunchasake project is quoted individually, so cancellation and refund terms are set out in the written proposal you accept before any work starts.

Before you accept a proposal

The first conversation and the proposal are free, and you are under no obligation to go ahead.

After you accept a proposal

If you want to pause or cancel a project, email hello@bunchasake.com. We will reply, confirm what has been delivered so far and settle the account according to the terms in your accepted proposal.

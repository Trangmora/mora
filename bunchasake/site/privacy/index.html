Privacy policy

Last updated 11 Nov 2026

This policy explains how Bunchasake handles personal information when you visit bunchasake.com or write to us.

Who we are

Bunchasake is built by Impact Investments S.R.L., VAT ID IT01331310324, Via Guido Zanetti 1, 34133 Trieste (TS), Italy, and operated by Mora AI Business (household business), Vietnam. You can reach us at hello@bunchasake.com.

What we collect

  • What you send us. When you email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you choose to tell us about your business.
  • Technical data. The website has no contact forms, no advertising trackers and no analytics cookies. Like any website, the servers that deliver it receive technical data such as your IP address and browser type.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you and prepare for a possible project with you.
  • To keep the website secure and working.

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Services that process data for us

  • Cloudflare hosts the website, protects it and forwards emails sent to hello@bunchasake.com to our mailbox.
  • Google provides the mailbox where those emails arrive, and the web fonts used on the site, which means your browser requests the fonts from Google's servers.

How long we keep it

We keep emails for as long as we need them to talk with you or to work on your project, and as required by law, for example for invoices.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop using it. Email hello@bunchasake.com. If you live in the European Union, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority; in Italy that is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date below.

Tell us your story.

Write us a few lines about your business. We read it, prepare our questions, and set up a 60-minute conversation.

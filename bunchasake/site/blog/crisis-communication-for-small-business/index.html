Step 06, Crisis communication: prepare before you need it

Every brand faces a communication crisis at some point. In the sixth step Bunchasake prepares scenarios, trains your team and stands by you when something happens, to keep the impact and the risk to your brand as small as possible.

By the Bunchasake team · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways
  • Most crises start with a small incident, a hasty reply and a few hours of silence at the wrong moment.
  • We prepare in advance: industry scenarios, spokespeople, reporting process and ready-made statements.
  • Your team is trained from the point of view of communication professionals.
  • When an incident happens, we research it and advise on the response with you.

Running a business always brings the unexpected: a complaint that spreads fast, a safety incident, an employee who posts the wrong thing. The bigger the company and the more touchpoints it has, the more risk. The Crisis communication step means you never have to choose between staying silent and speaking too fast.

What Bunchasake does in this step

  1. Mapping the situations that could happen. Based on your industry and model: restaurants, retail, fashion, tech, franchising.
  2. Building the response process. Who is told, how fast, who approves the reply, which channel responds first.
  3. Assigning spokespeople. And a backup when the main one is unavailable.
  4. Writing ready-made statements. For each type of situation, to adjust when used.
  5. Training your team. Managers and staff learn to spot the signs, give a first response and know when to escalate.

What you receive

  • A crisis playbook for your industry.
  • A reporting process and spokesperson roles.
  • Ready-made statement templates.
  • A training session for managers and staff, with an easy reference sheet.

Who is involved and what you approve

You choose the spokespeople and approve the whole playbook. When something happens, Bunchasake researches, advises on the response and stays with you; the final decision always rests with the business.

For chains and growing brands

For chains and franchise systems, an incident at one location can affect the whole system, because customers do not distinguish between branches. The playbook applies consistently everywhere, including franchise locations.

How this step connects to the others

This step closes the six-step process, so the work of the first five steps is not lost overnight. The step before it is step 05, Community; start again from step 01, Listening.

Frequently asked questions

We have never had a crisis. Do we need this?

Yes. Preparing when nothing is wrong is far cheaper and calmer than handling it in the middle of one.

Should we stay silent or respond when criticised online?

On social media, silence is not neutral. Acknowledge early and briefly, then find the facts before explaining. A prepared playbook helps you do that fast and right.

Does Bunchasake help while an incident is happening?

Yes. For clients with a playbook, we help research the incident and advise on the response; the final decision stays with the business.

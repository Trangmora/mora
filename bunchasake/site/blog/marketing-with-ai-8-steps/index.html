Marketing with AI in 2026 comes down to eight steps: research your customers, set up a website and social channels, build the setup inside an AI assistant, plan ideas and A/B test them, film real video for AI to edit, schedule and post automatically, use the data to decide, then add up the cost. This is how Trang, founder of Mora AI, markets brands today, as explained in the video at the top of this page. The video is in Vietnamese, so this article writes the eight steps out in full, with the cost table, so you can follow them without being technical.

The running example is a silk clothing brand. Swap in your nail salon, restaurant or shop and the method stays the same. To watch on YouTube, here is the 8-step video on the Trang AI channel.

Step 1: market research

This is the most important step, and no AI can do all of it for you. The goal is to learn what customers like and need.

  • Go to Facebook, TikTok and Instagram and find videos in your field that many people are watching. Study how they are made. Do not copy. Learn the approach, then create your own stream of content.
  • Save all the links in one place.
  • Read the comments. Customers ask things like "Is this colour still available?", "How much is it?", "Does this silk wrinkle?". Those questions tell you what customers need and roughly what they are willing to pay.

There are two ways to do this. The first is by hand, which is best when you are starting out. The second is a data collection tool such as Apify, connected to your AI assistant so it reads the videos and comments and summarises them for you. If you use a tool, collect only public content and read each platform's terms.

Step 2: build your "home" online

You should have a website, and optimise it so customers find you when they search Google or ask AI assistants such as ChatGPT. Those two jobs are called SEO and GEO, and we cover them in our guide to what GEO is. Then come the social channels: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X. Be present on the ones your customers actually use.

Step 3: build the setup inside an AI assistant

In the video, Trang uses Claude, an AI assistant that costs about 20 US dollars a month. If you work alone or run a small or mid-sized business, this setup can take over most of the daily design and writing.

Inside the assistant, you connect the tools you need through APIs. Think of an API as a cable that lets the assistant operate other tools on your behalf.

  1. Images. Connect an image generation tool, for example from Gemini or GPT.
  2. Video. Connect a video generation tool if you need one.
  3. Skills. For example a writing skill: you write one sample post in your brand voice, then teach the assistant to write the same way.

For the first five to ten posts you still have to check and correct everything carefully. How well AI performs depends on the data, instructions and rules you give it. Our guide to the AI marketing assistant for small business shows how to write those instructions.

Step 4: plan ideas and A/B test

From what you found in step 1, make two different versions of the same idea, post both, and see which one customers prefer. That is an A/B test. AI does the writing and the images. Your part is choosing the idea and reading the result. To plan a whole month, see our guide to the AI content calendar for small business.

Step 5: real video, edited by AI

A brand still needs real video, filmed in the shop or studio with a phone. Editing used to take hours for each video. Now you film, hand the footage to the assistant together with a skill that describes how you want it edited, and correct the first five or so videos with it. After that it edits in the right direction on its own.

Real images are what AI cannot replace. Customers buy because they see the real product and the real craft.

Step 6: schedule and post automatically

Connect the assistant to a posting platform. We use Morapost, a platform built by Mora AI. Once the AI has written the posts, it schedules the whole month and publishes to your social channels. Another platform works too. How to write one idea for several channels is in our guide to multichannel posting from one plan.

Step 7: use data for campaigns and customer care

Once content runs consistently, you start to have data, and that data is gold.

  • Sales data. If customers buy on your website, the assistant can track which items sell best. If the cream silk shirt sells three times more than other colours, you know where to put your content and your ads.
  • Campaigns. Run ads for the item that is selling, and discount at the right time for the right group of customers.
  • Customer care. The assistant reads messages and summarises them each week: how many customers complained, and about what. If ten customers ask about silk wrinkling, you know you need a video on washing and ironing.

AI reads the numbers and reports. Which campaign to run is still your decision. Never paste customers' names, phone numbers or private details into an AI tool.

So what is left for you to do

You do steps 1 and 2 yourself. You build step 3 once. From then on AI makes the content, edits the video and posts. You only give instructions, for example: "This week post three videos on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. Report back when done."

One thing not to forget: every three days and at the end of each week, review the results. Which videos were watched, which posts customers saved. Keep the kind of content that wins and drop what nobody watches.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping step 1. If AI writes before you have read your customers, every post comes out generic.
  • Not editing the first ten posts. AI learns from your corrections. Skip them early and it stays wrong.
  • Letting AI publish unread. Prices, promotions and product facts need a person to check them.
  • Using AI images in place of real ones. Illustrations are fine. Product pictures must show the real product.
  • Never reviewing results. Without the review, an A/B test means nothing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I do marketing with AI if I am not technical?

Yes. Steps 1 and 2 need no special technology. Step 3 is the hardest and is done only once; you can learn it from the video or have someone build it for you, then run it yourself.

How much does marketing with AI cost per month?

By the estimate in the video, the tools cost roughly 800,000 to 1,000,000 VND a month with AI images and real filmed video, or about 1,000,000 to 1,800,000 VND with AI-generated video added. Tool prices can change.

Do I have to use Claude?

No. The video uses Claude as the example because it is the tool Trang works with. The method applies to other widely used AI assistants, as long as they can connect to an image tool and a posting platform.

Will AI run my ads and answer every customer message by itself?

AI reads the numbers, summarises messages and answers repeated questions. Which campaign to run, how much to discount and how to handle a complaint are still decided by a real person.

How often should I review the results?

About every three days and at the end of each week. Look at which posts were watched and saved and what else customers asked, then keep the winning kind of content for the following week.

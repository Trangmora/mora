Step 05, Community: the most durable growth comes from regulars

A brand that grows without a community behind it is not yet a successful brand. In the Community step Bunchasake helps you build a customer community on the platforms your customers prefer, so they come back, recommend you and stay loyal.

By the Bunchasake team · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways
  • Customers who return and recommend others save a great deal of sales and marketing spend.
  • We build community on the platforms your customers actually like to use.
  • For chains, the community belongs to the brand, not to a single store.
  • For Vietnamese businesses abroad, community often has two layers: the Vietnamese community and local customers.

Ads bring customers in the first time. Regulars are the ones who come back, bring friends and stand by the brand when something goes wrong. A good community does not form by itself; it has to be designed, cared for and given a reason to belong.

What Bunchasake does in this step

  1. Choosing the right community platform. A Facebook group, Zalo, a brand-owned channel or in-person events, depending on where your customers are.
  2. Designing a loyalty program. Tied to the brand, usable at every branch, and more than discounts.
  3. Planning seasonal events and activities. Following each market's culture, for example Tết and the Mid-Autumn Festival for Vietnamese businesses abroad.
  4. Creating content customers want to share. Stories, details and experiences people want to tell their friends about.
  5. Guiding your team on community care. How to reply, how to remember regulars, how to thank people who refer others.

What you receive

  • A community plan: platforms, goals and owners.
  • A loyalty program for the whole system.
  • A seasonal calendar of events and community content.
  • Community care guidelines for your team.

Who is involved and what you approve

Bunchasake builds the plan and the guidelines; day-to-day community care is done by your team, because customers need to meet the real people behind the brand. You approve the plan before it starts.

For chains and growing brands

For chains, we design it so a regular at one branch feels welcome at another. For Vietnamese businesses abroad, we help connect two communities: Vietnamese customers looking for a taste of home, and local customers curious about the culture.

How this step connects to the others

The bigger the community, the more the brand needs protecting. Step 06, Crisis communication, prepares for the unexpected. Before it comes step 04, Reach.

See how we work with Vietnamese businesses abroad and Vietnamese brands going international.

Frequently asked questions

Does Bunchasake manage the community day to day?

We build the plan, program and guidelines. Daily care is done by your team, because customers need to meet real people. AI can help with reminders and summaries.

Is a loyalty program just a discount card?

Not only. Discounts bring customers back, but being remembered by name, invited first and thanked for referrals is what makes them feel they belong.

Does a B2B company need a community?

Yes, in a different form: a customer group, expert-sharing events, and the founder's content on LinkedIn.

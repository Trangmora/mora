Step 01, Listening: we start with the founder's story

Every Bunchasake brand project starts with a conversation, not a design. In the Listening step we find the brand story in the person who built the business, then write it up as a short summary for you to approve.

By the Bunchasake team · Published 11 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

Key takeaways
  • Listening is a 60-minute conversation with the founder, online or in person, after we have read what you send us.
  • We uncover four things: why the business exists, what you believe in, where the business stands now and how you want to be remembered.
  • You receive a plain-language summary of the brand story, core values and goals.
  • Once you approve the summary, we move on to Positioning.

Many mid-sized companies already have good products, loyal customers and growth. But the brand was often built in a hurry in the early years, while the founder was busy keeping the business alive. When it is time to expand, what is missing is rarely a prettier logo. It is clarity: who we are, why we exist and how we want to be remembered. The Listening step is where we find that clarity, together with you.

What Bunchasake does in this step

  1. Reading what you already have. Company profile, website, social pages, packaging, store photos, press coverage. We arrive with questions written for your business.
  2. A 60-minute conversation with the founder. Online or in person, in Vietnamese or English. Why you started, what you will never compromise on, which customers you remember most, and where you want the brand to be in ten years.
  3. Listening to the people who carry the brand. For companies with a team, we can also talk to the marketing lead or store managers, to see how the brand is understood inside.
  4. Capturing concerns, not just numbers. What worries you about expansion and what you are afraid of losing. These shape the next steps more than you might expect.
  5. Writing the summary. The story, values and goals on a few short pages, in your own words.

What you receive

  • A brand story summary short enough for a new employee to retell.
  • Core values, each with a real example from how the business already works.
  • Brand goals for the next stage: markets, customer groups and the image you want.
  • Open questions to carry into Positioning.

Who is involved and what you approve

The founder is the most important person in this step. If there are co-founders or a second-generation successor, we invite them too. After the conversation we send the summary for you to read, edit and approve. Nothing is designed on a guess.

For chains and growing brands

For chains and growing companies, Listening has one more job: capturing the soul of the first store before it gets diluted across branches. When the founder can no longer stand behind every counter, their story has to be written down so the manager of the fifth or tenth branch can tell it too.

How this step connects to the others

The approved summary feeds step 02, Positioning, where we analyse competitors to find where that story stands in the market.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the conversation?

About 60 minutes, online or in person. Beforehand we read what you send; afterwards we send the summary for you to approve.

What do I need to prepare?

Nothing formal. Send what you already have, such as a company profile, website and social links, store photos. The rest is answering honestly.

We already have a brand. Do we need this step?

Yes. For an existing brand, Listening shows whether the current identity still fits the story and goals, so you can decide what to keep and what to refresh, as in our Refresh package.

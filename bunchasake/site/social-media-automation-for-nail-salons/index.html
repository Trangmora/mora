A nail salon content calendar your staff can follow

A nail salon content calendar says what goes out on which day and who supplies the photo. Ours rotates five kinds of post, so the feed is never thirty pictures of the same French tip:

  • Nail art: the set you were proudest of this week.
  • Before and after: a repair, a fill, a pedicure.
  • Promotions: a quiet-day offer, a seasonal colour.
  • Staff: who does what, so customers can ask by name.
  • Customer care: how to make a set last.

Technicians take the photos on their phones. The assistant drafts each caption in English and Vietnamese, in your salon's voice. You edit or approve between customers, and the post is scheduled.

Nail salon social media management: we build it, your team runs it

Owners who search for nail salon social media management often expect an agency that posts for them every day. We do not do that, run ads or do photo shoots. We set up the system and your team runs it, and the accounts stay yours.

The inbox follows the same idea. We write automatic replies to booking questions you answer every day: opening hours, the price list, how to book. Replies point to the booking link or phone number you already use, and anything unusual goes to a person.

What each platform allows for scheduling and replies differs and can change, so we set up what your accounts support.

Questions, answered

Do you manage our Facebook and Instagram for us?

No. We set up the system and your own team runs it.

Can the AI post without asking me?

No. The AI drafts and a person approves. A post is scheduled only after approval.

Does it connect to my booking software?

We do not promise that. The automatic reply sends customers to the booking link or phone number you already use.

Can we get the automation without a brand package?

It is an add-on to our Start, Build or Refresh package, because the assistant needs a clear brand voice to learn. If you already have a brand, Refresh starts from what you have.

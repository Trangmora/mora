How social media automation for small business works

Most owners do not need someone to post for them forever. They need a system light enough to keep going. So we build this social media automation service once, around your brand, and then it is yours.

A normal week for your team:

  • The 30-day calendar already says what this week's posts are about.
  • The AI assistant drafts them in your voice, in English, Vietnamese or both.
  • One person reads each draft and approves it. Nothing is published without that yes.
  • Approved posts are scheduled automatically. Common inbox questions get an automatic reply, and complaints still go to a person.

What we do not do: post on your behalf every day, or run ads. Platform features vary by account and change over time, so we set up what your accounts support. See social media automation for nail salons and social media automation for restaurants.

Why Facebook, Instagram and TikTok automation starts with your brand

An AI assistant can only sound like you if it knows who you are. Without a clear brand voice it guesses, and your posts read like every other page in your industry.

That is why the automation is an add-on to the Start, Build or Refresh package. The brand work gives the AI what it needs:

  • Positioning: who you are for and how you are different. This shapes your calendar's themes.
  • Story and key messages: the things worth repeating, in your own words.
  • Voice and guidelines: how you speak and how your posts should look.

For Vietnamese owners abroad, our page on marketing for Vietnamese businesses overseas explains the brand side. To plan a month yourself, read our guide to an AI content calendar for small business.

Six steps, and you approve each one

Step 1

Listening

A 60-minute conversation to hear the founder’s story: why you started and what you believe in. We note the words you use.

Step 2

Positioning

Who the brand is, who it is for, how it differs. We analyse your competitors in depth to see where you stand and which way to go, guided by what the founder wants. Key messages become calendar themes.

Step 3

Identity

We work directly with the founder and the person in charge of the brand: logo, colours, typography and voice, agreed part by part in guidelines built for the long term. The AI assistant learns this voice.

Step 4

Reach

Once customers start to recognise the brand, we take it where they go: website, signage, cards, social media (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, LinkedIn, Zalo, Pinterest) and the media they follow. For existing channels, we read the data, customer behaviour and past campaign results, then advise on new campaigns. The automation is added here.

Step 5

Community

A brand that grows without a community behind it is not yet a success. Returning customers from a good community save a lot of sales and marketing spend, so we help you build one on the platforms your customers prefer, for loyal customers.

Step 6

Crisis communication

Every business meets a communication crisis at some point. Our team, specialised in studying and handling crises, keeps the impact and risk to your brand to a minimum and trains your team to respond like communication professionals.

Questions, answered

Do you post for us or run our ads?

No. We build the system and your own team runs it, approving each draft before it is scheduled. We do not post on your behalf every day and we do not run ads.

Can I get the automation without a brand package?

It is an add-on to Start, Build or Refresh, because the AI needs a clear brand voice to sound like you. Already have a logo you like? Refresh keeps what works.

Will the content be in English and Vietnamese?

Yes, if your customers need both. We work in Vietnamese and English. See our guide to bilingual marketing for Vietnamese businesses abroad.

Which tools do you use?

We do not name tools here: the right setup depends on your accounts and what your team finds easy. We only set up what your accounts support.

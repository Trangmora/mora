Restaurant branding that starts with the story

Most owners ask a restaurant branding agency for a logo first. We start with why the place exists. Picture a family phở restaurant: the broth is a grandmother's recipe from Nam Định, and the family opened abroad so their children would grow up with that taste. None of it is on the sign, the menu or the Instagram bio.

That story becomes the brand. It decides the name on the door, the colours and how the menu describes each dish:

  • Before: Phở bò. Beef noodle soup.
  • After: Phở bò Nam Định. Our family's broth, simmered overnight with beef bones, ginger and star anise.

We write the voice in two languages: English tells local diners what they are about to eat, Vietnamese keeps the warmth your community knows. A restaurant in Vietnam that wants foreign guests to understand its menu needs the same work.

Restaurant social media your own team can run

To be clear about what we do not do: we do not run ads, post every day on your behalf or do food photo shoots. A page written by outsiders rarely sounds like the person behind the stove.

Instead, we set up a system that makes posting a small job between lunch and dinner service:

  • post templates in your colours, ready for a photo from your phone
  • voice guidelines in English and Vietnamese, so captions sound the same whoever writes them
  • with the add-on, social media automation for restaurants: a 30-day calendar, AI drafts you approve, scheduled posts and automatic replies

Questions, answered

Why work with a Vietnamese restaurant marketing agency?

Nobody has to explain the food to us. We work in Vietnamese and English, so your story reaches local diners without losing its meaning.

Do you post for us, run ads or take food photos?

No. We build the brand, the templates and, as an add-on, the automation system. Your team takes the photos and posts.

We are in a different time zone. How does that work?

We work online between Vietnam and Italy, with clients anywhere. After a 60-minute first conversation, you approve each step before we move on.

Can we keep our current name and logo?

Yes. The Refresh package audits your current brand, then repositions it, refreshes the identity and updates the touchpoints you already use.

What does it cost, and what do we prepare?

The quote comes after the first conversation and depends on the package: Start, Build or Refresh. To begin, email hello@bunchasake.com with your story and your current menu.

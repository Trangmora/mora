Many companies already have brand guidelines, but they are often a thick file sitting on the design team's computer. Branch managers, new staff and print partners never open it. A year later every branch has a slightly different sign and every page a different voice. Our Identity step creates a beautiful identity and, more importantly, guidelines everyone can actually follow.

What Bunchasake does in this step

  1. Developing visual directions from the positioning. Every direction explains why it tells the story approved in step 02.
  2. Designing the logo and identity system. Logo, colour palette, typography, patterns and photography style. If needed, we advise on naming too.
  3. Writing the brand voice. How the brand addresses people, which words it uses, and sample greetings, thank-yous and apologies for different situations.
  4. Designing key touchpoints. Signage, packaging, business cards, social profile images and post templates, depending on your package.
  5. Packing it all into brand guidelines. With right and wrong examples, and the things never to do.

What you receive

  • A complete logo and identity system in formats for print and screen.
  • Brand guidelines: story, identity, voice and in-store experience.
  • Templates for key touchpoints.
  • A shared asset folder so everyone always uses the right files.

Who is involved and what you approve

The founder and brand lead work directly with us and agree on each part, from the first direction to the final detail. You approve the identity and guidelines before we take the brand to your channels in the Reach step.

For chains and growing brands

For chains and franchise systems, the guidelines are written so that people who are not you can still get it right, with sections for branch managers, franchise partners and print vendors. We also spell out what a branch may adapt locally and what must never change.

How this step connects to the others

With the identity in place, step 04, Reach, takes the brand to where customers look. Before it comes step 02, Positioning.

Frequently asked questions

Why not design the logo first?

A logo drawn before you know who you are, who you serve and how you differ may look good but tends to be replaced soon. The first two steps give every design choice a reason.

What do the brand guidelines include?

Story and core values, logo and usage, colour palette, typography, photography style, voice and sample lines, in-store experience, and the things never to do.

We want to refresh an old identity. Is that possible?

Yes, that is our Refresh package: we assess the current brand, keep what loyal customers love and renew the rest.

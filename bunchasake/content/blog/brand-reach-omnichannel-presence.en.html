Being on ten channels and doing each half-heartedly is worth less than doing three channels that bring customers really well. The Reach step helps you pick the right channels, give each the right role, and run them consistently without burning out your team.

What Bunchasake does in this step

  1. Reading your channel data. Reach, engagement, messages, reviews, branded searches and past campaign results.
  2. Studying customer behaviour. Where your customers find you, where they decide, and how they recommend you.
  3. Giving each channel a role. For example: Google Maps is where customers decide, Instagram where they feel the brand, LinkedIn where partners build trust, the website where everything is confirmed.
  4. Planning content and campaigns. One brand story, told the way each channel works.
  5. Building an AI-assisted operation. Scheduling across channels, answering repeated questions in the brand voice, and a short weekly report. Every public post is approved by a person.

What you receive

  • An assessment of your current channels and where to focus.
  • A channel map: the role of each channel and how they connect.
  • A content plan and strategic advice for the next campaigns.
  • With our AI Growth Partner service: an automated system for scheduling, messages and reporting.

Who is involved and what you approve

You and your marketing team review the data with us, choose priority channels and approve the content plan. Bunchasake advises on strategy and builds the system; paid ads are run by your team or your ad partner.

For chains and growing brands

For chains, we design a model where head office keeps the brand and branches keep the closeness: one shared content calendar with local parts for each branch, one voice guide for the whole chain, one inbox for messages from every channel, and a weekly report comparing branches.

Frequently asked questions

Does Bunchasake run ads?

We advise on strategy and build the content system; paid ads are run by your team or ad partner. We build the foundation that makes every ad dollar work harder.

Will AI replace my marketing team?

No. AI handles repetitive work like scheduling, common questions and reporting. The story, the point of view and the final decisions stay with your team.

We have no channels yet. Where do we start?

We help you pick the two or three channels that matter most to your customers, usually Google Maps, a website and one main social channel, then grow from there.

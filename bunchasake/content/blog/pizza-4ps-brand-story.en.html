In 2011, Yosuke Masuko, a Japanese entrepreneur, and his wife left their corporate jobs to open a small pizza restaurant in Saigon. More than a decade later, Pizza 4P's runs over 40 locations across Vietnam, Cambodia, Japan, Indonesia and India (Pizza 4P's). What built the brand is not only good pizza, but the way one idea guides everything they do.

Starting with a reason to exist, not a menu

"4P's" is short for "for Peace": Pizza for Peace. The company vision is one line: "Make the World Smile for Peace" (Pizza 4P's vision page). It sounds big for a pizzeria, but that line helps them answer hard questions: which ingredients to use, how to treat staff, how to grow.

This is positioning at its deepest. Guests remember more than the food; they remember the feeling the brand sets out to give.

Turning ingredients into a story

In 2012, Pizza 4P's opened its own cheese workshop in Don Duong district, Lam Dong, near Da Lat, to make cheese for its restaurants. Vegetables and other ingredients also come from local farms. This farm-to-table approach is not only about quality: it became part of the brand story, something guests can see, taste and tell others about.

Experience is the strongest marketing channel

In many Pizza 4P's restaurants, guests can see the kitchen and the oven like a stage. The space, the service, even the table setting stay consistent with the idea of bringing a smile. When the experience is special enough, guests take the photos, write the reviews and bring their friends back. That is marketing advertising can hardly buy.

Growing one market at a time

Pizza 4P's opened its first restaurant abroad in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, in 2021. In December 2023 it opened its first store in Japan (Inside Retail Asia), reached Indonesia in late 2024, and then Bengaluru, India. In each place the story stays the same: house-made cheese, local ingredients and the "for Peace" vision.

Lessons for growing chains

  • Write your reason to exist first. One short line everyone on the team knows helps every branch make the same decisions. This is the step of listening to the founder's story.
  • Let your sourcing tell the story. Ingredients, craft and farms can all become honest content, with no need to exaggerate.
  • Invest in the in-store experience. For a restaurant chain, every meal is a post your guests write for you. See our restaurant branding guide.
  • Grow slowly and surely. Each new market needs time for the story to take root, just as Cong Ca Phe kept its Hanoi memory intact abroad.

Frequently asked questions

When and where was Pizza 4P's founded?

In 2011 in Ho Chi Minh City, by Japanese entrepreneur Yosuke Masuko and his wife.

What does "4P's" mean?

It is short for "for Peace". The brand's vision is "Make the World Smile for Peace".

Can a small chain learn from Pizza 4P's?

Yes. You don't need your own cheese workshop, but any chain can write down its reason to exist and let it guide the menu, the space and the service.

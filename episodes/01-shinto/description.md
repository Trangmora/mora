**Title:** Japan's 8 Million Gods: What Is Shinto? | Asian Myths & Culture

**Description:**
Japan has eight million gods, but where do they come from? Shinto has no founder and no holy book. It began with rice farmers who thanked the mountain, the river and the storm. In this episode: the sun goddess Amaterasu hiding in her cave, the torii gates of Fushimi Inari, why sumo wrestlers throw salt, the scholar who became the god of exams, and the shrine at Ise that is rebuilt every 20 years.
